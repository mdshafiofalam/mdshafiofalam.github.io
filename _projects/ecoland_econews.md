Our team has designed and developed a serious game focused on climate change, environmental awareness, sustianbility practice, climate education through user action named Ecoland: Econews. The initial Ecoland game concept was created by Anjalee Wanigarathne (consultant of the project) for her master's thesis. We received the project as a conceptual game with backend story, and game portions designed in Figma for user testing for the thesis. From there, we expanded the game concept according to the project clients' (Dorina Rajanen and Mikko Rajanen from [INTERACT Research Group](https://interact.oulu.fi/), University of Oulu) requirements, created the whole game play with story line, and implemented the functional game in Unity.

Our work, had two phase, in first page, we expanded the game concept by reviewing scientific literature related to gamification, climate change information dissemination, user-centered design, sustainability, and game engine selection. We expanded it by including primary and secondary game loops with multiple game progression concepts such as time-shift and episodic, and added Econews, a text annotation mini-game with the “Citizen Science” concept (a major client requirement). To test, all the new modifications, we created a hi-fi Figma prototype, and connecteUser Experience (UX) and Usability Evaluation course. In the second phase, after creating the Figma prototype, we implment a functional version of the game in Unity engine.

The game has two major compoents: one is the story mode with episodic and time shift concept. The other one is minigames with infinite game concept. In our work, one of the major requirement was to implement the Econews minigame feature for climate related news annotation from YLE (Finnish Public Finnish Broadcasting Company)'s online news site for research data collection. Here, we applied citizen sceice concept to engage public with the game for collect research data. We added a red panda nammed Rory (an endangered species) for rhetoric building, emotional connection, engagement, and fun. In future, interation Rory the red panda can be used as the avater for conversational AI agent for user engagement.
