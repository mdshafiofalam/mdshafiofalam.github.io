Our team has designed and developed a sustainability-related serious game named Ecoland: Econews. The Ecoland game concept was created by Anjalee Wanigarathne as part of her Master's thesis. From there, we expanded the game concept according to the project clients' (Dorina Rajanen and Mikko Rajanen) requirements.
In our work, we have expanded the game concept by reviewing scientific literature related to Gamification, climate change information dissemination, user-centered design, and game engine selection. We expanded it by including primary and secondary game loops with multiple game progression concepts such as time shift and episodic, and added Econews, a text annotation mini-game with the “Citizen Science” concept, which was a major client requirement. First, we created a Figma prototype based on the necessary changes, then we developed the functioning version of the game in the Unity game engine.
