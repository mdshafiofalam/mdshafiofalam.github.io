Developed a mobile app concept to alter and persist online privacy and security behavior. The app starts from providing the latest best practices of online security and privacy hygiene. It follow the persuasive system design principal so that the user experience is unobtrusive with autonomy. In addition, the app also gradually changes the user behavior to a healtheir one therough behavior change support system.
