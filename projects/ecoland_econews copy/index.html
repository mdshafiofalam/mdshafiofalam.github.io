I led a team of five members to investigate the usability and user experience of a VR-based meditation system with a Meta Quest 3 headset, with its companion mobile app paired with a Muse EEG headband, and collated data for the project customer. We conducted expert evaluation, then searched & selected appropriate Usability methods from the literature for the VR-based system VRSUQ (Virtual Reality System Usability Questionnaire) and the mobile app SUS (System Usability Survey). Designed a testing method for the usability evaluation and data collection; later recommendations were drawn for future improvement based on the analysis results.
