I enjoy exploring how users' characteristics and surroundings shape their interactions with technology and designing interactive systems that account for these factors, giving users **greater control** over technology for **empowerment**. Currently, I am interested in **designing technology for sustainability**, and want to pursue a research career in HCI. Check out my [research works](/research/). You can call me either **MD** or **Shafiul**, I am OK with both.

I have completed Masters in **Information Processing Science (IPS)** from the [University of Oulu](https://www.oulu.fi/en), Finland. I took the **Informaiton Systems Orientation**, and have completed: **Human-Centered Design & User Experience** and **Persuasive Design & Gamification** specializations. For, my masters thesis through the **Sustainable Human-Computer Interaction (SHCI)** lense, I have explored community sustainability from data center operation  supervised by [Prof. Marianne Kinnula](https://mariannekinnula.net/). Here, I developed human-centric sustainable data center establishement and operational guidelines to ensure local community sustainability is ensured from data center operation by collecting inputs from Oulu city residents. Parmanent home of my [MS thesis](https://oulurepo.oulu.fi/handle/10024/63656). I also hold a Bachelor in **Computer Science & Engineering** from [Daffodil International University](https://daffodilvarsity.edu.bd/), Bangladesh.

I am interested in designing and devloing computing system based on **humane values for just, sustainable future**. For this, I conduct exploratory study to understand the end-user needs, then design and build systems with end-user, and conduct user studies for fine-tuning. From, my previous studies, I have expereince in conducting user studies with various methods and system development. Check out my [projects](https://mdshafiofalam.github.io/projects/).
