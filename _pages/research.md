### Data Center Operation and Community Sustainability

For my MS thesis, I conducted a qualitative, exploratory study with residents of Oulu city to capture their voices on the sustainability of their community in relation to the data center operation. I placed my work within **Sustainable Human-Computer Interaction (SHCI)** domain, and collected empirical data using **Semi-structured** and **Participatory Design**. Later, analyzed it using **Braun and Clarke’s Thematic analysis** framework, to unpack data center's indentity in the eyes of community members, their needs, benefit and vision for a sustianble data center operation. The findings were used to inform data center operation guidelines to ensure local community's sustainability in economic, social, and environmental aspects. This guideline can be used by the government to inform policy and data center companys' to plan out their project. The study's findings also contributed to the SHCI literature by introducing human-centric data center design concepts, and bringing the data center discussion to the HCI community.

### Undergraduate Students' Knowledge on AI Sustainability in Low-resource Environment

In this project, I have collaborated with researchers from Bangladesh. Here, we conducted a qualitative study of Computer Science students on their knowledge, perceptions, and practices regarding AI sustainability concepts. We unpacked their learning methods realated to AI and sustainability concepts, then we advocated for inclusion of sustainability concepts in the AI courses' classroom and made suggestion for curriculumn modifications based on students practices and expectations.
